Pursuing mechanical engineering at Dibrugarh University with a deliberate focus on thermal science — thermodynamics, heat transfer, and the systems that move heat from where it is to where it can do work.

Reads every machine as an energy balance: boiler, condenser, compressor, cooling tower — the quiet components that decide whether a design survives contact with the second law.

Drafts in AutoCAD — schematics, layouts, dimensioned heat-exchanger geometry — because a thermal calculation still has to become a drawing before it becomes metal.

Follows manufacturing closely: grinding, near-net-shape processing, precision casting and forging — the processes that set the tolerances a thermal design must live inside.
